Meet artificial intelligence

AI is technology that performs tasks that usually need human abilities—such as recognising an image, understanding language, finding patterns or making predictions.

Important: AI does not have a human mind. It uses patterns from data to calculate a likely output.

SECTION 2 · 4 MIN

AI vs normal software

Traditional software follows rules written by a person. Machine-learning software finds useful patterns in examples.

Normal software

Rules + input → answer

A calculator follows exact instructions. The same input always produces the same answer.

Machine learning

Examples + training → model

A spam filter learns patterns from labelled emails, then predicts whether a new message is spam.

SECTION 4 · 4 MIN

AI and generative AI around us

Predictive AI ranks, recognises or forecasts. Generative AI creates new text, images, audio or code by predicting what should come next.

Recommendations

Streaming services predict what you may enjoy from viewing patterns.

Navigation

Maps predict traffic using current and past journeys.

Recognition

Phones match face or speech patterns.

Generation

Chatbots predict words to create a response—not retrieve guaranteed truth.

SECTION 5 · 5 MIN

When confident answers are wrong

AI is strong at speed, pattern-finding, drafting and generating options. It can still invent facts, citations or events. These mistakes are called hallucinations.

Misinformation check: pause, find the original source, compare reliable sources, check the date and ask for evidence.

SECTION 6 · 5 MIN

Bias, fairness and privacy

AI can copy unfair patterns in its training data. Fair systems need relevant, representative data and human checks. Privacy matters because prompts may be stored or reviewed.
